# ClearPaper
A practical initiative to develop standardized, open-source documentation templates aligned with EU sustainability regulations (CSRD, CSDDD, EUDR), enabling both importers and upstream producers to meet legal obligations without relying on expensive consultants or guesswork.

This project supports regulators, cooperatives, SMEs, and certifiers by turning policy text into actionable, localized, and testable formats — starting with the coffee sector.



## Why Now?
- EU compliance requires documentation that currently has no universal format.
- Fragmentation across member states creates a bureaucratic nightmare for importers and producers.
- Without reliable templates, actors at the bottom of the supply chain are pushed out or forced to fabricate documentation.

---

## What We’re Building
- **Template Library**: Pre-built, editable documents that translate EU legal obligations into structured, fillable forms.
- **Legal-Mapped Fields**: Each data field is directly traceable to its legal source (article/section of CSRD, CSDDD, etc.).
- **Multi-Format Outputs**: Available in Word, PDF, LaTeX, and JSON — usable on paper or through automated systems.
- **Localized Versions**: Templates translated and adapted to national language, legal terminology, and cultural context.
- **Validation Layer**: Legal and academic institutions (e.g. Windesheim UAS) verify alignment with national implementation.

---

## Initial Use Cases
- Coffee cooperatives and exporters preparing EUDR deforestation documentation
- SME importers needing CSDDD-aligned due diligence reports
- NGOs helping producers comply with CSRD reporting at origin
- Customs authorities reviewing shipments against inconsistent paperwork

---

## Pilot Goals
- Deliver **5 core templates** by Q4 2025 for CSRD, CSDDD, and EUDR
- Test templates with **3 cooperatives and 3 EU importers**
- Align formats with **2 certifiers** and **at least 1 EU customs authority**
